# Beryllium-7 in classical novae

Reproduction data for *Ionization corrections to ⁷Be abundances in classical novae: New spectroscopy of V1405 Cassiopeiae and a reanalysis of three other novae*.

Download and extract [V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip](V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip). Start with `manuscript_reproduction/README.md`, which indexes the 22 figures and 16 tables by figure or table number and manuscript label. The archive includes four electronic observation and line tables, figure inputs and scripts, and selected Cloudy inputs and outputs.

**Matching manuscript version.** The 26 November 2026 revision includes the V1405 Cas high-velocity profile comparison (Figure 7) and the five Ca III recombination coefficient trials in section 7.1 and appendix 8. The archive contains the HVC fit inputs, trial results and plotting scripts, and the Cloudy inputs, numerical outputs and checked summary for the five recombination trials. The manuscript data citation links to the exact snapshot.

Raw Subaru/HDS frames are available from [SMOKA](https://smoka.nao.ac.jp/). The observation log gives the dates and instrumental settings.

Verify the downloaded ZIP against [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS).
