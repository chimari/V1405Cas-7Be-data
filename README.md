# Beryllium-7 in classical novae

Data and figure reproduction for *⁷Be in classical novae: New measurements in V1405 Cas and revised abundances in three other novae*.

Download and extract [V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip](V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip). Start with `manuscript_reproduction/README.md`, which indexes the individual figures and tables by their manuscript labels. Each subdirectory describes its data, scripts, and dependencies. Most scripts regenerate figures or tables from saved analysis outputs.

- The archive includes the manuscript reference graphics and the electronic line tables, including the four-nova beryllium mass fractions in Table 8.
- Figure 18 uses the final snapshots of nine V339 Del Cloudy runs for panel (c). The earlier three runs are retained as the inputs to the Saha comparison in panel (b).
- Raw Subaru/HDS frames are available separately from [SMOKA](https://smoka.nao.ac.jp/). See the manuscript observation log for the observing dates and instrumental settings.

`SHA256SUMS` records the checksum of the downloadable archive.
