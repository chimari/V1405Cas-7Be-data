# Beryllium-7 in classical novae

Reproduction data for *Ionization corrections to ⁷Be abundances in classical novae: New spectroscopy of V1405 Cassiopeiae and a reanalysis of three other novae*.

Download and extract [V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip](V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip). Start with `manuscript_reproduction/README.md`, which indexes the 21 figures and 16 tables by their manuscript labels. The archive includes the four electronic observation and line tables, the figure inputs and scripts, and selected Cloudy inputs and outputs needed for the analysis. The appendix entries include the V339 Del +38 d spectrum and the UV025 B1 calcium rates.

**Matching manuscript version:** the 25 November 2026 revision responding to the second round of review (`V1405Cas-PASJ-rereview-edit-20261125`). This archive contains the figures and tabulated results cited by that version, including the updated Table 4 caption and the daughter lithium abundances in the Table 12 note. Use the GitHub commit permalink in the manuscript's data citation to retrieve this exact snapshot.

Raw Subaru/HDS frames are available from [SMOKA](https://smoka.nao.ac.jp/). The observation log gives the dates and instrumental settings.

Verify the downloaded ZIP against [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS).
