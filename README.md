# Beryllium-7 in classical novae

Reproduction data for *Ionization corrections to ⁷Be abundances in classical novae: New spectroscopy of V1405 Cassiopeiae and a reanalysis of three other novae*.

Download the [base reproduction archive](V1405Cas-7Be-reproduction.zip) and the [27 November Fe/H update](V1405Cas-7Be-FeH-update-20261127.zip). Extract the base archive first, then extract the update into the same directory and replace the matching files. Start with `manuscript_reproduction/README.md` in the merged directory. It indexes 23 manuscript figures, 16 tables, four electronic tables and three supplementary figures.

**Matching manuscript version.** The 27 November 2026 revision includes the Fe/H and total hydrogen-column scans discussed in sections 6.4, 8.2 and 9 and Appendix 9. The archive contains Figure 23, the three comparison-nova diagnostics, updated Tables 6, 8, 11 and 12, and the Cloudy input packages and selected numerical outputs for all four novae. It also retains the high-velocity profile comparison (Figure 7) and the Ca III recombination trials (Appendix 8). The manuscript cites the exact snapshot.

The supplementary plots can also be viewed directly for [V339 Del](supplementary_figures/V339Del_FeH_NH_diagnostics.pdf), [V5668 Sgr](supplementary_figures/V5668Sgr_FeH_NH_diagnostics.pdf), and [V2944 Oph](supplementary_figures/V2944Oph_FeH_NH_diagnostics.pdf). Their source CSVs and scripts are in `manuscript_reproduction/comparison_nova_feh_nh_sensitivity/` inside the ZIP.

Raw Subaru/HDS frames are available from [SMOKA](https://smoka.nao.ac.jp/). The observation log gives the dates and instrumental settings.

Verify both downloaded ZIP files against [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS).
